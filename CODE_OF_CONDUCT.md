# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

PdfiumRaster contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation welcoming, respectful, and
constructive. Examples include giving actionable feedback, respecting differing experience and viewpoints, and
keeping technical disagreements focused on the work.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private
information, and other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, release communication, and other project-related channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, and whether harmful behavior is repeated.

To report a conduct issue, open a GitHub issue asking the maintainer for a private contact channel without including
sensitive details. If there is an immediate platform-safety concern, use
[GitHub's abuse reporting tools](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
